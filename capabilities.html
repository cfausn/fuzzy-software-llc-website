Capabilities

Four practices, one standard of work.

Our capabilities are designed to work independently or together. Each engagement is shaped around the organization we are working with.

01

Advisory & Architecture

We provide independent technical perspective to leadership and engineering teams. Our work clarifies the current state of a system, identifies meaningful risks and opportunities, and charts a practical path forward.

  • Technical assessments and due diligence
  • System and solution architecture
  • Modernization and migration planning
  • Security, reliability, and operational review
02

Software & Platform Engineering

We design, build, and maintain software across the full stack, from customer-facing applications to the infrastructure and data systems beneath them. Our emphasis is on clarity, maintainability, and systems that teams can own with confidence.

  • Application and product development
  • Cloud infrastructure and DevOps
  • Data platforms and analytics
  • Quality engineering and test automation
03

Staff Augmentation

We provide experienced engineers who integrate directly into client teams, adopting existing tools, processes, and culture. Arrangements range from a single specialist to a dedicated team.

  • Individual engineers and technical leads
  • Dedicated, embedded teams
  • Specialized and hard-to-hire skill sets
  • Flexible arrangements that adapt over time
04

Game Development

We develop interactive experiences, bringing the same engineering discipline we apply elsewhere to the craft of games. We work on original projects and in collaboration with partners.

  • Game design and prototyping
  • Gameplay, engine, and tools engineering
  • Production and release support
  • Multiplayer and online services
