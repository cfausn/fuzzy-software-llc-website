About

A technology firm built by practitioners.

Fuzzy Software brings together engineers, architects, and creators who share a commitment to careful, durable work.

The firm

We began as a small group of senior engineers. Today we serve a wide range of clients across advisory, engineering, staffing, and interactive entertainment.

Our clients range from early-stage companies to established enterprises. Some come to us with a specific problem. Others want a long-term partner to extend their capabilities. In each case, our approach is the same: understand the context and do the work well. We leave things better than we found them.

We value quiet competence over spectacle. Most of our work is never seen publicly, and we prefer it that way.

Security & confidentiality

Trust is foundational.

We treat client systems, code, and data with the highest standard of care.

  • Mutual non-disclosure agreements as standard practice
  • Least-privilege access, requested only as needed
  • Intellectual property transfers to the client per agreement
  • Client code and proprietary information are never reused or shared
  • Responsible use of AI tooling, with human review and client consent
Working with us

Straightforward to engage.

We can work under a client's master services agreement or provide our own standard terms. Our team is based in the United States and works across time zones to stay aligned with the teams we support.

Every engagement begins with a conversation to understand goals, constraints, and fit.
